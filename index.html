Mandatory 3 Signs of BIS Hallmarking

As mandated by the Bureau of Indian Standards (Govt. of India), all hallmarked gold jewelry sold must carry three distinct laser-engraved marks. ANSH Assaying & Hallmarking Centre strictly adheres to this standard.

SIGN 1 OF 3

BIS Standard Mark

The official triangular mark representing the Bureau of Indian Standards. It certifies that the precious metal has passed rigorous purity parameters under national standards.

Statutory Guarantee of Quality
SIGN 2 OF 3

Purity in Karat & Fineness

Indicates the precise gold ratio in parts per thousand: • 22K916 (91.6% Pure Gold) • 18K750 (75.0% Pure Gold) • 14K585 (58.5% Pure Gold)

Standardized for all official transactions
SIGN 3 OF 3

6-Digit HUID Code

Hallmarking Unique Identification (HUID). Every single jewellery article is given an individual 6-digit laser-etched alphanumeric fingerprint uploaded directly to the BIS national portal.

Prevents counterfeiting & duplication
Government Consumer Protection & Statutory Compliance

Consumer Protection Guide & Live HUID Verification

Under BIS (Hallmarking) Regulations, every gold jewelry article must carry a verifiable 6-digit laser HUID code. Verify your article directly below against the national hallmark database.

BIS National Portal API Gate Active

1. Check 3 Inscribed Signs

Verify that the jewelry carries: BIS Triangle Logo, Purity Mark (e.g., 22K916, 18K750), and the 6-digit laser-etched HUID code. Do not accept gold without all 3 marks.

2. Verify via "BIS Care" App

Open the official BIS Care mobile app, select "Verify HUID", enter the 6-character code, and view the certified jeweller name, assaying lab ID, weight, and hallmarking date.

3. 2x Compensation Rights (Rule 49)

Under BIS Act Rule 49, if any hallmarked gold article is found to have lower purity upon testing at a recognized centre, the buyer is entitled to 2 times the purity difference + testing fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions about Assaying & Hallmarking?

Everything you need to know about BIS Hallmarking compliance, testing methods, sample sizes, and consumer rights.

HUID stands for Hallmarking Unique Identification. It is a 6-digit alphanumeric code assigned uniquely to each gold article tested and hallmarked at a BIS recognized center. The code is uploaded to the central BIS database, providing an immutable audit trail that prevents fake stamps, under-karat jewellery, and fraud. Consumers can verify it instantly via the BIS Care app.
XRF (X-Ray Fluorescence) is a rapid, non-destructive surface analysis that takes 60–120 seconds and causes zero damage to ornaments. Fire Assay (Cupellation) is a chemical process (IS 1418) where a tiny scraped sample is melted at 1100°C to separate base metals and silver using nitric acid. Fire Assay is the ultimate legal benchmark for purity certification under BIS guidelines.
Yes! Consumers and individuals are welcome at ANSH Assaying & Hallmarking Centre. You can walk in with your jewellery, get a non-destructive XRF purity assessment or Fire Assay report, and receive an authentic testing certificate. The nominal government-regulated fee makes it very affordable.
Standard turnaround time is within 4 to 6 hours for jeweller batches inwarded before 12:00 PM. Express same-day service is available for priority lots. Walk-in XRF testing for consumers is completed within 15 to 30 minutes.
If an article declared as 22K (916) tests below the mandatory tolerance limit, it cannot be hallmarked as 22K. As per BIS regulations, the batch is either down-marked to its actual verified lower grade (e.g. 18K or 14K) upon written consent from the client, or returned un-hallmarked with a detailed non-compliance assay failure report.
