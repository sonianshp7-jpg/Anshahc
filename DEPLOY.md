# ANSH Assaying & Hallmarking Centre - Production Launch Guide

Your website is **100% production-ready** for immediate public launch on any domain or hosting provider.

---

## 🚀 Option 1: Launch on Netlify in 30 Seconds (Easiest & Free)
1. Open [https://app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) in your browser (create a free account if needed).
2. Drag and drop your **`d:\ANSH\website`** folder into the browser box.
3. Your website is instantly live with a free SSL certificate (`https://your-site-name.netlify.app`)!
4. *(Optional)* Connect your custom domain (e.g. `www.anshhallmarking.com`) in **Domain Management**.

---

## ⚡ Option 2: Launch on Vercel (Free & High Speed)
1. Go to [https://vercel.com/new](https://vercel.com/new).
2. Select **Deploy Existing Project** or connect via GitHub.
3. Choose the `d:\ANSH\website` folder.
4. Click **Deploy**. Your site will be live instantly on Vercel's global CDN with automatic HTTPS.

---

## 🌐 Option 3: Launch on GitHub Pages (100% Free Forever)
1. Create a repository on [GitHub](https://github.com) named `ansh-hallmarking`.
2. Push or upload all files from `d:\ANSH\website`.
3. Go to **Settings** > **Pages** > Select branch `main` and root `/` > Click **Save**.
4. Your website will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/ansh-hallmarking/`.

---

## 🏢 Option 4: Traditional CPanel / Hostinger / GoDaddy Hosting
1. Log in to your hosting **cPanel** or **hPanel**.
2. Open **File Manager** and navigate to `public_html/`.
3. Upload all files from `d:\ANSH\website` into `public_html/`.
4. Your website is immediately accessible on your domain!

---

## 🔒 Security & SEO Included
- **Google SEO & WhatsApp Previews**: Open Graph metadata, Twitter Cards, and schema.org JSON-LD are pre-configured.
- **Search Engine Indexing**: `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` are included.
- **Admin Shield**: `admin-login.html` is protected with `noindex, nofollow` to prevent search engines from crawling the login page.
- **Mobile PWA**: `site.webmanifest` allows clients to bookmark or install the website like an app on mobile devices.
